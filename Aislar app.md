*MOTHER'S DAY HISTORY:*

*How it started:*

After her mother, Ann Reeves Jarvis, died in 1905, Anna Jarvis wanted a special day to honor mothers for their love and sacrifices. In 1908, she organized the first official celebration. In 1914, Woodrow Wilson officially declared Mother’s Day a national holiday in the United States.

In Nigeria, like most countries of the world, Mother’s Day is celebrated on the second Sunday of May.
Since it falls on Sunday, Many churches in Nigeria celebrate it specially during Sunday services, where mothers are appreciated and sometimes given gifts or prayers. Families also often celebrate by appreciating their mothers with messages, visits, or small presents.

*MOTHERING DAY HISTORY:*

*How it started:*

Mothering Day (or Mothering Sunday) is an old Christian tradition that started in England. Hundreds of years ago (around the 16th century), people in England had to attend their “mother church” once a year during the Christian season of Lent. The "mother church" means the main church or cathedral where a person became born again and was baptized or belonged.
On that Sunday, people travelled back to worship there. Because many workers and servants lived far from home, this church visit gave them a rare chance to visit home. When they returned home. They visited their mothers and families just to use one stone to kill two birds since they have been away from home for a long time.
They sometimes brought small gifts or a special cake called Simnel cake to their mothers and families. Over time, gradually, some Christians turn it into a day to appreciate mothers also and some turn it into another mother's day.

Mothers are worth celebrating every day, I have no problem with that but I think I need to clarify this confusion for people who are willing to learn.
